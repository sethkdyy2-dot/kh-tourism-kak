Koh Rong

The Island
Paradise.

Koh Rong is Cambodia's premier island destination, famous for its glowing plankton, white sands, and crystal-clear turquoise waters.

Covering approximately 78 square kilometers, Koh Rong offers a diverse range of experiences—from the lively party atmosphere of Koh Touch to the serene, untouched beauty of Long Set Beach and Lonely Beach. It is the perfect place to disconnect and immerse yourself in nature.

Marine Life & Adventure

The waters around Koh Rong are home to vibrant coral reefs, making it a hotspot for snorkeling and diving. After dark, head to the calmer beaches to witness the magical bioluminescent plankton that light up the sea like stars.
