Heritage Site 01

Siem Reap

The Soul of the
Kingdom.

Angkor Wat is the heart and soul of Cambodia. As the largest religious monument in the world, it stands as a testament to the architectural genius of the Khmer Empire.

Built in the early 12th century by King Suryavarman II, the temple was originally dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu before transitioning into a Buddhist site. Its intricate bas-reliefs and iconic towers are recognized globally as a masterpiece of human creativity.

Beyond the Main Temple

The Angkor Archaeological Park covers over 400 square kilometers. Don't miss the enigmatic faces of Bayon Temple or the jungle-entwined ruins of Ta Prohm, made famous by its massive tree roots reclaiming the stone.
